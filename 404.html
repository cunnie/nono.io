404 Page Reaped

The page that you seek has either expired or moved on to another plane of existence. It was mortal, as are all things. It has returned to the pool of electrons from which it once was shaped into the page that brought you such pleasure and happiness. Our own moments in the light are ephemeral and uncertain, just as this page's was.
